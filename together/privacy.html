Together — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Together keeps your trip plan private by default. No ads. No tracking SDKs.

Itinerary edits, notes, and preferences stay on your device (local storage). Optional Firebase sync, if enabled in a configured build, may store shared plan state — it is not required to use the app.

Navigate and booking links open Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Safari under those services’ policies. Together does not sell personal data.
